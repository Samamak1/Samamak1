## Start here

### [Restaurant Crew Scheduling Engine](https://github.com/Samamak1/crew-scheduling-engine)

A representative reconstruction of scheduling tools I used to turn availability, qualifications, training requirements, coverage rules, and labor targets into posted weekly schedules for a 43-person team. The engine produces an 11-tab workbook and makes each assignment rule inspectable.

### [RYGNeco Client Reporting Prototype](https://github.com/Samamak1/rygneco-reporting-engine)

A product prototype connecting pickup, inventory, testing, disposition, exception, and client records to governed reporting. I defined the reporting problem, taxonomy, workflow, requirements, and acceptance criteria while coordinating technical contributors.

### [Restaurant Launch Program](https://github.com/Samamak1/restaurant-buildout)

The operating case behind a 300-seat restaurant opening. I coordinated buildout, licensing, vendors, systems, menu design, staffing, training, payroll, and launch readiness. The concept generated $165,000 in its first 14 days and served up to 600 covers nightly.

### [Alcohol Pricing Decision System](https://github.com/Samamak1/alcohol-pricing-engine)

An invoice-to-POS pricing model for hundreds of beverage items. It standardizes cost per ounce, pour cost, markup, approval, and product-mix review without exposing supplier pricing.

### [Trade with MAK Subscription Program](https://github.com/Samamak1/mak-trading-case-study)

The operating system behind a financial education community that reached about 2,000 members and more than 1,200 cumulative paid subscribers. I led acquisition, onboarding, curriculum, member support, recurring delivery, payments, platform migration, and closeout. The paid program operated from 2019 through 2023.
